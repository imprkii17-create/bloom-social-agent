How much does a streaming model earn? An honest answer

The short answer: with us, €2,000+ a month is a realistic goal, but earnings vary and aren't guaranteed. The longer answer is about what they depend on, and how to spot an agency that promises too much.

The short answer

With us, €2,000+ a month is a realistic goal, and you keep up to 80% of what you bring in. Earnings vary and aren't guaranteed: in the same month, two models can earn very different amounts. That's why we don't promise a specific figure for your first month, and you shouldn't believe another agency that does.

What do streaming model earnings depend on?

  • Your hours. About 20 hours a week on average, in 5-hour blocks. Regular shifts count for more than a few long, occasional ones.
  • Regularity. Returning viewers are the basis of your income, and a predictable schedule keeps them coming back.
  • Your audience. It is still growing in the first weeks, so your first months don't show where you could be later.
  • Mood and conversation. Viewers come back for your personality.

How does revenue share work?

Streaming platforms usually take their own share of what viewers spend. What remains is shared between the model and the agency. With us, you keep up to 80% of what you bring in.

Whichever agency you talk to, ask in writing: exactly what percentage is yours, and what it is calculated from, the platform's payout or total revenue. The same percentage can mean a very different amount depending on which one it is.

How and when we pay

Payouts are monthly, and you always see exactly what you earned and when the payment arrives. Training is free.

What to expect in the first months

The first weeks are about learning and building your audience: finding your style, your schedule and your viewers. Your manager helps you see what works.

Over time you can reach the point where you work almost independently. That can mean more freedom and higher earnings, with our support still behind you.

Tax

Earnings are taxable. Ask an accountant about the right setup for you, and ask the agency what kind of contract you would work under.

When is a promise a red flag?

  • A specific, large amount is promised for your very first month.
  • They say the money is certain, no matter how much you work.
  • You don't get the percentage and the payout terms in writing.
  • They ask for money upfront for training, equipment or "registration".

If any of these is true, ask more questions or look for another agency. What a trustworthy studio looks like is in the webcam model job guide, and the differences between home and studio work are here: from home or in a studio.

Frequently asked questions

How much does a new streaming model earn?

There's no single number. Earnings depend on your hours, how regularly you stream and your audience, so they vary and aren't guaranteed. With us, €2,000+ a month is a realistic goal, and you keep up to 80% of what you bring in.

How often do you pay?

Monthly, and you always see exactly what you earned.

Will I definitely reach €2,000 a month?

Nobody can honestly promise that. It's a realistic goal, but earnings vary, and a lot depends on you.

Do I have to pay for training?

No, training is free.
