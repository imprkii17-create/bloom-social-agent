Why the studio works

Our studio is in Budapest's 5th district, near St. Stephen's Basilica: central, easy to reach, and spacious inside. Professional lighting, reliable equipment, and staff who keep everything running, so all you need to bring is yourself.

Many people choose the studio because it keeps work and home completely separate. You don't have to rearrange your room or worry about someone walking in, and at the end of the shift you simply go home.

Why home works

No commute, and you work in surroundings you know. We help you set up a home studio: lights, camera and settings, and getting started online. Training and the team's support are the same as in the studio.

What you need to stream from home

  • A stable internet connection. A cable is more reliable than Wi-Fi.
  • A quiet room you can lock, where nobody disturbs you during a shift.
  • Good light. One or two soft light sources matter more than an expensive camera.
  • A tidy background that shows nothing personal: photos, letters, a recognisable view from the window.
  • A computer and a camera. We help you work out exactly what you need before you start.

Privacy at home: before you go live, check what the camera can see. Nothing should point to your address or your real name.

Not from Budapest?

You have two options: work from home, or come to the studio in Budapest. If you choose the studio, we help arrange travel and accommodation, even for longer stays.

How to decide

If keeping work and home separate matters to you, or you don't have a quiet room of your own, the studio is the better choice. If you live far away, or feel most at ease in your own space, start from home.

Either way the schedule is flexible: about 20 hours a week on average, in 5-hour blocks, planned around you. Read more about the work in the webcam model job guide, and about earnings here: how much a streaming model earns.
