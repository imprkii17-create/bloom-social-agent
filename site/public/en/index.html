Questions

Online modeling jobs: straight answers before you apply.

The things people ask us most. Anything else? Ask us in your application and we'll answer honestly.

What does a streaming model actually do?

You host live online video streams, from our studio or from home. You chat with your audience and set the mood, and you choose the style — glamour, lingerie, lifestyle or something else. What you show, and when, is always your decision.

Do I need any experience?

No. We train you from your very first stream: setup, lighting, pacing and building an audience that comes back. All you need to bring is confidence and enthusiasm.

How much can I earn?

€2,000+ a month is a realistic goal, and you keep up to 80% of what you bring in. Earnings depend on your hours, engagement and other factors, so they are never guaranteed. Payouts are monthly, and you always see exactly what you earned.

How many hours do I need to work?

About 20 hours a week on average, in 5-hour blocks. Morning, afternoon, evening or night shifts — we plan the schedule together with you.

Where will I work?

In our studio in Budapest's 5th district, a few steps from St. Stephen's Basilica, or from home — we help you set up a home studio. If you're not from Budapest, we help arrange travel and accommodation.

How do you protect my privacy?

You decide what is visible and to whom, and your personal details stay private. If your content shows up somewhere it shouldn't, we help you file a takedown request (DMCA).

Who can apply, and what happens next?

Anyone aged 18 or over. The form takes two minutes and needs one photo. We usually reply within 72 hours, and before you start we verify your age and identity.

What this page is

This explains what happens to the information you submit through our application form.

What we collect

Your name, email, phone number, age, your answers in the application, an optional referrer name, and a photo you upload.

Why we collect it

Only to review your application and get in touch about next steps. We don't use it for anything else.

Who processes it

Our application form is handled by Formspree, Inc., a third-party form-processing service based in the United States. Submitting the form sends your information to Formspree on our behalf, solely so it can be delivered to us.

How long we keep it

If your application doesn't move forward, we delete your details — including your photo — within 12 months. If you join us, your information is kept as part of your work records for as long as that relationship requires.

Your rights

You can ask us at any time to show you what we have on file, correct it, or delete it, by emailing bloommodelsapply@gmail.com. You can also contact Hungary's data protection authority, the NAIH, if you believe your data has been mishandled.

Age

We only accept applications from people 18 or older, and may ask you to verify your age and identity before you begin any work with us.
