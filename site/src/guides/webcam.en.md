## What is a webcam model job?

A webcam model (also called a cam model, streaming model or online model) hosts live video streams on an international streaming site. Viewers talk to them in the chat, and the model builds an audience through personality, mood and conversation. The income comes from viewers' support, which the platform and the agency also take a share of.

Three names, one job: "webcam model", "streaming model" and "online model" mean the same thing. Agencies use different words, but the work is the same: a live, online presence on camera, in your own style.

> **In short:** it's for adults 18+ only, you don't need experience, and you always decide what you show and to whom.

## What does a shift look like?

With us, an average week is about 20 hours, in 5-hour blocks. We plan the schedule together with you: morning, afternoon, evening or night, whichever fits your life best.

1. **Setup.** Lights, camera, sound. In the studio the team handles it; at home we help you set it up.
2. **Going live.** You chat with viewers and set the mood and the topic.
3. **Building an audience.** Returning viewers matter most, and a regular schedule and a personal tone keep them coming back.
4. **Feedback.** Your manager helps you see what works and what's worth changing.

## Do you need experience?

No. It's fine if you've never streamed before: we train you from your very first stream. We show you the setup, the lighting, the pacing, and how to build an audience that comes back to you. What actually matters is confidence, reliability, and enjoying a good conversation.

## How much can you earn?

With us you keep up to 80% of what you bring in, and €2,000+ a month is a realistic goal. Earnings vary and aren't guaranteed: they depend on your hours, how regularly you stream and how your audience grows. Payouts are monthly, and you always see exactly what you earned. More detail: [how much a streaming model earns](/en/streaming-model-earnings/).

## Studio or home?

Both work. Our studio is in Budapest's 5th district, near St. Stephen's Basilica, with professional lighting and equipment. If you'd rather work from home, we help you set up a home studio. A side-by-side comparison: [online modeling from home or in a studio](/en/online-modeling-from-home/).

## Privacy and safety

Many people worry that someone they know will find out. That's why privacy is built in from the start:

- You decide what is visible and to whom.
- Your personal details stay private.
- If your content shows up somewhere it shouldn't, we help you file a takedown request (DMCA).
- Before anyone starts work, we verify their age and identity.

## How to choose a webcam studio you can trust

Plenty of studios and agencies recruit in Budapest. Before you apply anywhere, ask these questions:

| Question | A good answer |
|---|---|
| Will I get the terms in writing? | Yes: a contract or written agreement you can read at your own pace. |
| Exactly what share is mine, and what is it calculated from? | A clear percentage, and whether it comes from the platform's payout or from total revenue. |
| When and how am I paid? | Regular, fixed payouts and a statement of what you earned. |
| Do I have to pay anything upfront? | No. A serious agency doesn't charge for training or for starting. |
| Do they check age? | Yes, for everyone. A studio that doesn't care about your age is a warning sign. |
| What if I want to stop? | Clear, fair terms for leaving, with no pressure. |

**Warning signs:** a promised or unrealistically high amount for your first month, pressure to sign quickly, upfront fees, or no written terms.
