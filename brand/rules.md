# Bloom Models — content rules

These rules are checked automatically (`bloom validate`) and, if Claude is switched on, by Claude (`bloom review`).

## Must
- Every post is clearly for adults: mention **18+** in the caption.
- Captions in Hungarian first, then English, separated by "—".
- **At most 5 hashtags** (Instagram's limit since December 2025).
- Earnings: say "up to 80%" and "€2,000+ a month is a realistic goal"; always add that earnings vary and aren't guaranteed.
- Describe the work as "streaming model" / "online modeling" (HU: "streamelő modell", "online modellkedés").
- Images: JPEG, 4:5 portrait (1080×1350) for feed posts, 9:16 (1080×1920) for stories and Reels.

## Never
- Words like webcam, adult, sexy, nude, OnlyFans (and the Hungarian equivalents) in captions, alt text or hashtags.
- Sexually suggestive images or wording.
- Fake testimonials or invented reviews.
- Engagement bait: "tag a friend", "comment YES", "like if…".
- Promises of guaranteed income.
- Mentioning an upper age limit.

## Voice
Warm, confident, discreet. Short sentences. Speak to one person ("you"). Practical facts over hype.
